01 · Why

Research landscapes are hard to read from the outside.

Capabilities are spread over thousands of labs, facilities and programmes, each described differently and most of them out of date. Decisions run through principal investigators, facility managers, committees, tenders and funding rounds, often all at once. Market reports count citations and survey sentiment; very few show who can actually do what, who decides, and what happens next.

Portolaan reads the landscape from the inside: years of running research infrastructure, working with instrument makers and shaping research policy, combined with an AI-maintained map that stays current.

08 · About

Named after the charts that opened trade routes.

I'm Yuyang Wang. I work where research infrastructure, laboratory technology and research policy meet.

My background is in optical microscopy. Over the past years I have run research facilities and instrumentation, worked closely with instrument makers on installation, service and development, and helped shape national and European research infrastructure initiatives.

I build and maintain two open maps of self-driving laboratories, one global and one for China, and I work in English and Chinese. Portolaan is where that view of the landscape becomes useful to others.

09 · Questions

Before we talk.

Do you sell or represent products?

No. Portolaan advises; it does not resell, represent or take commission on any product.

No. Clients come from anywhere. Chinese is one working language among others, alongside English and the European research systems themselves.

Research infrastructure broadly, with depth in laboratory automation, self-driving labs, characterisation and materials research.
